## 1. Product North Star

The controlling product intent remains:

> **Phase H is the AI market-interpretation safety belt, not a historical-market research engine.**

Phase G prevents AI from grounding analysis in unreliable or unauthoritative evidence.

Phase H prevents AI from misinterpreting reliable market evidence because trading status, corporate-action price-basis changes, reference-price context, recent scale, timing, or coverage are missing.

TW-Market remains an evidence infrastructure layer. It supplies governed facts, timing, coverage, missing-state semantics, citations, lineage, audit, and deterministic interpretation guards. AI/Agent remains responsible for natural-language analysis and explanation.

## 2. Frozen anti-scope-creep boundary

Phase H completion MUST NOT require or introduce:

- historical all-market database;
- durable OHLCV warehouse;
- daily history collector;
- automatic historical backfill;
- background crawler or refresh loop;
- scheduler or polling;
- full corporate-action archive;
- complete adjusted-price engine;
- technical-analysis engine;
- backtester;
- ranking / recommendation / trading logic;
- broker credentials;
- a seventh MCP tool.

Execution remains explicit, target-scoped, data-need-scoped, time-bounded, authorized, execute-once, and auditable.

## 5. Mandatory versus non-blocking scope

### 5.1 Mandatory for Phase H closure

1. **H2 real production evidence**
   - at least one official quote-relevant corporate-action route is active;
   - target binding, lifecycle stage, missing semantics, coverage and citations remain governed;
   - source limitation never becomes a false no-event claim.

2. **H3 real production evidence**
   - at least one governed bounded 1..20 completed-session route exists for each market claimed safety-complete;
   - N-day comparison proves N lookback observations plus one governed end observation;
   - partial/insufficient/unavailable/source-failed states remain distinct;
   - no persistence/backfill workaround is introduced.

3. **H4 production safety**
   - real H2 and H3 evidence reaches the deterministic safety gate;
   - no_material_discontinuity_detected is reachable only when relevant H2 coverage is affirmatively complete for the comparison window;
   - otherwise ordinary raw-return interpretation is blocked or caveated.

4. **H5 production projection**
   - H2/H3/H4 state reaches Result V3, Audit V3 and AI handoff without opinion or causal fabrication.

5. **Conversation-first acceptance**
   - real or governed-fixture scenarios prove that Phase H prevents the H0-defined interpretation errors.

### 5.2 Explicit non-blockers when truthfully exposed

The following do NOT block Phase H closure by themselves:

- complete H1 route coverage;
- TWSE and TPEx using identical transport;
- TWSE TWT49U optional licensed integration;
- automated capital-reduction coverage;
- par-value/split/reverse-split/share-consolidation source gaps;
- complete corporate-action archive;
- guaranteed complete 20D coverage on every request;
- full adjusted-price engine.

A source gap is acceptable only when applicability, uncovered scope and interpretation guards remain explicit.

~~~

If no governed portable default H3 route can be proven for a market, that market MUST NOT be represented as H3 safety-complete. The Owner may either:

2. approve a narrower market-specific Phase H closure;
3. separately change Roadmap scope.
